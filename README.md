### Morph: Revived Addon - RPG (Fabric/NeoForge)

Fabric \& NeoForge 1.21.1, 26.2, and 26.3



* Adds a more RPG style feel to how Morph works
* Changes Biomass into Mana



* Adds 2 new items
--Book of Morphs - Keybinds to Morph are now disabled and the only way to Morph is via this book, found in Dungeons
--Mana Crystal - When you Right Click you are given 15 mana, these can be crafted via 4 Glowstone Dust, 4 Blaze Powder, and 1 Amethyst Shard
